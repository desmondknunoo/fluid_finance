# Public finance site

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Ghanaian financial publication inspired by Bloomberg's compact markets pages. Real quotes and research lead; oversized marketing navigation and soft decorative panels give way to editorial hierarchy.

OWN-WORLD: Fluid's supplied mark, black utility bar, white or charcoal paper, strong sans-serif headlines, fine vertical dividers and heavy section rules. Cyan identifies actions; green/red identify signed price changes. Both themes are equally supported through a masthead toggle.

STORY: Scan market direction, open a company or research story, compare movers, then explore FX, fuel, discussions and education. No staff portal links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact brand masthead and horizontal topic navigation above a quote tape. A large market headline and data chart occupy the lead column, with research and a latest feed beside them. A session-status row communicates timestamps and source limits.

FORM: User-pinned Bloomberg financial editorial reference; code-led adaptation of existing React/Tailwind components. Keep live data and honest unavailable states.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
